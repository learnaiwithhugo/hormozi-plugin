# Naming

## Why it matters
A Grand Slam Offer makes no money if nobody hears about it. Naming determines how well advertising converts, response from outbound, and inbound organic responses.

"Free Six-Week Stress Release Challenge" vs "Float Tank Center Session." Same thing. You respond to the first.

## Offers fatigue, names refresh them
In a local market you can reach 1,000 people for about $20, so 200,000 people costs $10,000 to reach once. Offers eventually fatigue.

**Important disclaimer (p138):** reaching an audience once does NOT mean the offer is fatigued. Most people don't notice an offer on the first mention. Make new creative, new hooks, new stories around the same offer. Fatigue is a matter of years, not months.

**We are not changing the offer. We are only changing the wrapping paper.** (figure p139: "Same bundle, different wrapper")

## The M-A-G-I-C formula (p138-142, figures p138 and p139)
| Letter | Component | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | **Magnet** - make a magnetic "reason why" | Tells people the reason you're running this promotion |
| **A** | **Avatar** - announce your avatar | Calls out exactly who it's for and who it isn't |
| **G** | **Goal** - give them a goal | Articulates the dream outcome |
| **I** | **Interval** - indicate a time interval | How long the result will take |
| **C** | **Container** - complete with a container word | The word that makes it a bundle of things, not a commodity |

**Important note (p139):** not all components are mandatory. You'll typically use **three to five**. If you fit them all in, the name usually becomes too long.

### Component examples (p140-141)
- **Magnet (reason why):** Free, 88% off, Giveaway, Spring, Summer, Back To School, Grand Opening, New Management, New Building, Anniversary, Halloween, New Year
- **Avatar:** be as specific as possible, but no more. In a local area, the more local the headline, the better it converts. "Not Baltimore but Towson, MD. Not Chicago, but Hinsdale."
- **Goal:** Pain Free, Celebrity Smile, 1st Place, Never Out Of Breath, Perfect Product, Grand Slam Offer, Little Black Dress, Double Your Profit, First Client, High Ticket, 7 Figure, 100k
- **Interval:** AA Minutes, BB Hours, CC Days, DD Weeks, Z Months, "4 Hour", "21 Day", "6 Week", "3 Month"
  - **Careful:** on most ad platforms a quantifiable claim with a stated duration reads as a guarantee and will get disapproved. "$10,000 in 10 days" is risky where "Make Your First Sale in 10 Days" is not.
- **Container:** Challenge, Blueprint, Bootcamp, Intensive, Incubator, Masterclass, Program, Detox, Experience, Summit, Accelerator, Fast Track, Shortcut, Sprint, Launch, Slingshot, Catapult, Explosion, System, Getaway, Meetup, Transformation, Mastermind, Launch, Game Plan, Deep Dive, Workshop, Comeback, Rebirth, Attack, Assault, Reset, Solution, Hack, Cheatcode, Liftoff

### Two extra levers (p142)
- **Rhyme:** good rhymes stick. "Six-Pack Fast Track", "5-Day Book Print Sprint", "Marriage Thrive Deep Dive", "12-Week 2-Putt Shortcut", "12-Month No-Debt Reset", "Celebrity Butt Shortcut", "Get Some Ass Masterclass". Not a requirement, a nice-to-have. Don't force it.
- **Alliteration:** all or most words start with the same letter or sound. Easier for most people than rhyming. "Make Money Masterclass", "Change Your Life Challenge", "Big Booty Bootcamp", "Debt Detox", "Real Estate Reset", "Life Coach Liftoff".

## Pro tip (p144)
**Use the MAGIC formula for each item in your stack and bundle**, not just the offer. It automatically enhances the perceived value of every piece.

## What to change when offers fatigue, in order (p144, highlighted)
1. Change the **creative** (images and pictures in your ads)
2. Change the **body copy** in your ads
3. Change the **headline** - the "wrapper" of your offer (e.g. Free 6 Week Lean Challenge → Free 6 Week Tone Challenge; Holiday Hangover → New Year New You)
4. Change the **duration** of your offer
5. Change the **enhancer** (your free or discount component)
6. Change the **monetization structure**, the series of offers and price points (Book II)

Work top down. **Once you've monetized an offer, rarely should you change it. Just rinse and repeat.** Changing the machine creates operational drag and costs money.

## Naming summary (p146)
Half-assed naming can ruin conversions. Follow the steps and watch the same offer get 2x, 3x or 10x the response rate.
