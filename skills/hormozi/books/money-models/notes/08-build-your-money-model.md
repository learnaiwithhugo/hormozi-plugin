# Build your Money Model

**Use this when the question is about the whole offer sequence, not about the next single offer.**

## The four steps (p164-165, figure p164)

**Step 1) Start with an Attraction Offer.**
Goal: turn strangers into customers and cover your costs. Pick from Win Your Money Back, Giveaways, Decoy Offers, Buy X Get Y Free, Pay Less Now or Pay More Later. Then advertise it.
> "Figuring out what works best may take up to a year." (p164)

**Step 2) Pick an Upsell Offer.**
Goal: get 30-day profits well above the cost of getting and delivering to a new customer.
> "once you solve a problem, another appears. Those problems also need solutions. You solve the problems your Attraction Offer creates with Upsell Offers." (p164)
Pick from The Classic Upsell, Menu Upsell, Anchor Upsell, Rollover Upsell - **and make the offer at their time of greatest need.**

**Step 3) Pick a Downsell Offer.**
Goal: get the people who said no to say yes to something else.
- Keep the price the same, change **how they pay** → Payment Plans or Trials
- Charge less, change **what they get** → Feature Downsells
> "And best of all, you can alternate between them in the same sale. The more flexible you make your offers, the more people will buy them." (p165)

**Step 4) Pick a Continuity Offer.**
Goal: one last sale inside the 30-day window, plus stacked recurring cash. Continuity Bonuses, Continuity Discounts, Waived Fee Offers.
> "Sometimes the best timing for Continuity Offers happens after the first thirty days, and that's OK. It's better to make the offer at the right time than to try and force it at the wrong time." (p165)

## The staging rule - the part people ignore and pay for

> "Do not try and implement a full Money Model at once. It will break your business." (p167)

> "Perfect One Offer At A Time. It's tempting to implement a whole Money Model at once. Don't. Stick to your stage. Pick one offer. Try it. Keep doing it until it works reliably. Then, after it's reliable, do it so many times it gets automatic. Then, go to the next stage." (p165)

> "Patience is still the fastest way to get to your goal. So you'll need to measure in quarters, not weeks. You either build it right or you build it again. And again. And again. Building again - no matter how fast - still takes longer than building it right the first time." (p166)

**And the honest warning about what success does to you (p161):**
> "when your Money Model starts working, your business starts breaking. Part of the game."

## The two rules that stop you starting new businesses by accident

> "Simple Scales. Fancy Fails. Get as much as you can out of what you have. Remember, it's less about having 100 products to offer, and more about having 100 ways to offer your product. Think more ways to sell the same thing, not more things to sell." (p166)

One product, many offers: personal training becomes one, two, three or four sessions a week.

**Affiliate products fill the gaps without the delivery headache (p166, marked IMPORTANT in the book):**
> "In short, you can always offer somebody else's stuff in your Money Model."
- A dental agency sends clients to a braces manufacturer and takes commission
- A massage therapist sells someone else's home tools, and that company ships them
- An educator recommends a software and takes a commission per sign-up

## Two structural tricks

**Turn an Attraction Offer into a Continuity Offer with automatic renewal (p166).** A Buy 6 Months Get 6 Months Free offer that rolls into a month-to-month subscription at the end of 12 months gets the benefits of both. "A small tip with big implications."

**Mix and match freely (p167).** "You can use Upsell tactics in your Attraction Offer. You can install a Downsell process with every offer. You can use a Continuity Offer to attract new customers. There are no rules... start with the way I suggest it. Then, as you get better, experiment."

## Worked examples, straight from the book

### Gym Launch, services (p162)
| Stage | Offer |
|---|---|
| I. Attraction | **Decoy Offer** - free DIY decoy vs $16,000 Done-With-You licensing |
| II. Upsell | **Classic Upsell** - "Gym Lords," $42,000/yr ($36,000 prepaid) for advanced business services |
| II. Downsell | **Payment Plan Downsell** - seesaw from $10,000 down spread over 52 weeks, final offer $800/week for 52 weeks |
| III. Continuity | **Menu Close + Feature Downsell** - full package $800/wk, peelable into DFY advertising $300, sales training $200, monthly releases $500, original materials with tech support $100. Minimum package $100/wk |

The revenue trail he gives (p160): the Decoy Offer alone took Gym Launch to **$476,000/month in three months.** Adding Classic Upsell + Continuity Bonus + Payment Plan Downsell + Continuity Discount took it to about **$1,500,000/month.** Adding Menu Upsells + Feature Downsells took it to **$2,300,000/month, all within 14 months.** With Prestige Labs integrated, **$4,400,000/month by month 20.**

**Author Note (p161):** "When I started, I didn't know any of this Money Model stuff. It only looks clean looking back."

### Micro gyms, local business (p163)
- **I Attraction:** Win Your Money Back - pay-to-enter fitness challenge
- **I Downsell:** Payment Plan Downsell - split pay, three-pay, then Free Trial With Penalty
- **II Upsell:** Menu Upsell - supplement bundles personalised to the goal
- **II Downsell:** Feature Downsell - big bundle to small bundle to monthly subscription
- **III Continuity:** Rollover Upsell + lifetime discount, $50 off per month for life on a 12-month commitment

### Newsletter, digital product (p163)
- **I Attraction:** Free Trial - $0 then $399/month after 30 days
- **II & III:** Pay Less Now / Pay More Later + lifetime discount - **"Pay $297 Now and Keep That Rate For Life"**

> "I love this offer. It's nasty. It combines free trial, pay less now/pay more later, lifetime discount, and is an attraction offer, an upsell offer, and a continuity offer. A six-headed money-making monster." (p163, Author Note)

### Dog food, physical product (p164)
- **I Attraction:** Buy X Get Y Free - buy four months of food, get two free
- **II Upsell:** Classic Upsell - monthly? dog toys? dog vitamins?
- **II Downsell:** Feature Downsell - "Just the premium food then? You don't want anything else do you?"
- **III Continuity:** automatic renewal month-to-month after the first bulk purchase

## The whole book on one page (p169, figure p169)
1. A Money Model is a series of offers designed to increase **how many customers you get, how much they pay, and how fast they pay it.**
2. A **good** Money Model makes more profit from a customer than it costs to get and service them in the first 30 days.
3. A **$100M** Money Model makes more profit from one customer than it costs to get and service *many*, in 30 days - removing cash as a limiter to scale.
4. Money Models have four offer types: Attraction, Upsell, Downsell, Continuity.
5-8. The twelve offers, each in one line (p170-171):

| Type | Offer | One line |
|---|---|---|
| Attraction | Win Your Money Back | set a goal, tell them how to reach it, refund or credit if they do |
| Attraction | Giveaways | chance to win a big prize for contact info, then everyone else buys it discounted |
| Attraction | Decoy Offer | advertise free or discounted, present a more valuable premium beside it |
| Attraction | Buy X Get Y Free | free stuff in exchange for buying other stuff |
| Attraction | Pay Less Now or Pay More Later | full price later, or a discount plus bonuses now |
| Upsell | The Classic Upsell | "You can't have X without Y!" |
| Upsell | Menu Upsell | "You don't need that... you need this." |
| Upsell | Anchor Upsell | most expensive first, then the cheaper acceptable alternative |
| Upsell | Rollover Upsell | credit previous purchases toward the next offer |
| Downsell | Payment Plan Downsell | same product, same price, some now and the rest over time |
| Downsell | Trial With Penalty | "If you do X, Y, Z, I'll let you start for free." |
| Downsell | Feature Downsell | lower the price by changing what they get |
| Continuity | Continuity Bonus | an awesome free thing if they sign up today |
| Continuity | Continuity Discount | free time, now or later, if they sign up today |
| Continuity | Waived Fee | a startup fee, waived if they commit |

9. **You build Money Models one stage at a time.**

> "A $100M Money Model eliminates cash as a bottleneck for growth. Mission accomplished." (p167)
