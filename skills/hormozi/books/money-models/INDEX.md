## Two things to say out loud when they matter

**This book has no highlighted passages.** $100M Offers and $100M Leads carry them and they are a real signal; this one doesn't, and that is not a judgment on the material. Rank material here on Alex's own emphasis instead: the Author Notes, the "Highest Value Per Word Note In This Book" (p149), and the Summary Points at the end of every chapter.
