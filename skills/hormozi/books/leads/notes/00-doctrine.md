# Doctrine

## The one-line model
You have to sell stuff to make money. You need the stuff (an offer), people to sell it to (leads), and a way to get them to buy (sales). *$100M Offers* is the stuff. **This book is the people.** (p1)

**Advertising is "the process of making known."** (p1) That's the whole definition. It is not branding, not creativity, not copy. It is telling strangers the stuff exists.

## The problem, stated plainly (p15)
> "You're not getting as many leads as you want because you're not advertising enough. Period."

You only grow a business two ways: **get more customers, or make them worth more.** This book is entirely the first one. (p15)

**Highlighted (p15):** "You get more customers by getting; 1) More Leads 2) Better Leads 3) Cheaper Leads 4) Reliably (think 'from lots of places')."

> "All else being equal... when you double your leads, you double your business." (p16, figure p16)

## Lead vs engaged lead - the distinction the whole book hangs on
**Highlighted (p24):** "lead is a person you can contact." That's all. Bought list, phone contacts, people on the street - all leads.

**Highlighted (p24):** "engaged leads: people who show interest in the stuff you sell."

> "Engaged leads are the true output of advertising." (p24, figure p23)

He couldn't title the book "engaged leads" because nobody would get it. But that is the actual subject. **Anyone counting raw leads is measuring the wrong number.**

## The Core Four (p45-47)
Two audience types x two ways to communicate. That's it. There is nothing else one person can do.

| | People who know you (warm) | People who don't (cold) |
|---|---|---|
| **1-to-1 (private)** | Warm Outreach | Cold Outreach |
| **1-to-many (public)** | Post Free Content | Run Paid Ads |

- **Warm** = people who gave you permission to contact them: friends, family, followers, current and previous customers, contacts.
- **Cold** = people who have not: strangers, other people's audiences, bought lists, paid platform access.

> "These are the only four things you can do to let other people know about the stuff you sell." (p48)

> "So if you aren't getting as many leads as you want, you're not doing the core four with enough skill or with enough volume." (p48)

**Automation does not create a fifth category.** Emailing 10,000 people "once" is still one-to-one, done fast by a machine. (p47)

## The second layer: Lead Getters (p171-176, figures p171, p175)
The Core Four is *you* telling people. Lead getters are *other people* telling them. Four kinds:

| Lead getter | Its key strength (p245) |
|---|---|
| **Customers** (referrals) | biggest potential for low-cost exponential growth |
| **Employees** | your direct influence, run the business on your behalf |
| **Agencies** | teach skills you keep forever and transfer to your team |
| **Affiliates** | once going, can operate entirely on their own |

You cannot "do" affiliates or "do" referrals. **You do the Core Four to get them, then they do the Core Four for you.** (p175) The stack repeats: lead getters can go get lead getters.

The leverage ladder (p173-174, figures p173, p174):
1. You get leads. Work HIGH, leads LOW.
2. You get a lead getter. Work LOW, leads LOW.
3. You get lots of lead getters. Work HIGH, leads HIGH.
4. **You get a lead getter who gets lead getters.** Work LOW, leads HIGH. That's the $100M machine.

## What order to do them in (p169)
> "after I do warm outreach to get my pool of customers going - if I have more time than money, I move to posting content. If I have more money than time, I go with cold outreach or running ads."

Paid ads go **last** for two reasons: the skills from the other three transfer into it, and it costs money you'll have from the other three. (p156)

**But you only need one.** "So, just pick one. Then, max it out. Do more. Do better. Do new." (p169) They compound: a business doing content *and* ads gets more out of both than a business doing either alone.

## The beliefs underneath
- **Volume negates luck.** The whole book is one argument for it. "You can make up for your lack of skill with volume." (p119)
- **Advertising always works; only efficiency differs.** "advertising always works, it's only a matter of efficiency. So once you start, keep going until it works." (p242)
- **Highlighted (p73):** "If someone is making more money than you, they are better at the game of business in some way." Followed by: **"None of those beliefs make you better"** (p73) - about the excuses (rich parents, easy market, broke a moral code).
- **The many-sided die** (p266-268, figure p266). You can't see how many sides your die has. Green sides compound. The only two guarantees: the more you roll the better you get, and if you quit you lose. **"You cannot lose if you do not quit."** (p268)
